# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Job seekers — primarily mid-career professionals and recent graduates actively applying for roles. They are in a focused, goal-oriented headspace: they have a CV, they have a target job, and they want to close the gap between the two as quickly as possible. Context is a laptop at home or in a coffee shop, often during a concentrated job-search session.

## Product Purpose

Progrize is an AI-powered career platform. Users upload a CV (PDF or DOCX), Groq AI parses it into a structured profile, and they can then paste any job description to receive a fit score, ATS compatibility breakdown, and a tailored CV optimized for the role. Supporting features include application tracking, job discovery, and a community layer. Success means a user applies to a job with a better-matched CV and gets a callback they wouldn't have gotten otherwise.

## Brand Personality

Precise, confident, actionable. The tool does real work — it doesn't just visualize data, it moves users toward outcomes. Tone: direct and capable, not cheerful or playful. Voice of a sharp career advisor who gives you the honest truth and a clear next step.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS template aesthetics: hero with gradient mesh, big metric cards, rounded everything
- Resume.io / Canva resume builder vibes: pastel, decorative, soft
- LinkedIn: cluttered, corporate, social-feed monotony
- "AI productivity tool" purple-on-dark with glowing accents

## Design Principles

1. **Signal over noise.** Every element competes for attention during a high-stakes task. Strip ruthlessly.
2. **Confidence through precision.** Exact numbers, sharp type, tightly controlled spacing. Vagueness reads as incompetence.
3. **Progress is the product.** The UI exists to move the user one step closer to a job offer. Never celebrate process, only outcomes.
4. **Earn trust before asking for effort.** The first impression should communicate competence, not features.
5. **Clarity at speed.** Users in job-search mode are anxious and impatient. Interfaces must be scannable and decisive.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA as a baseline. Reduced motion support for animated elements. Sufficient color contrast for muted secondary text.
